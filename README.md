# Omkar's Mumbai

A 3D playable portfolio of Omkar Khandalkar, product designer from Mumbai. Play as Omkar, follow the marker to recover 7 case studies, ride the kaali-peeli taxi or the bike, fix bugs by jumping on them, and beat Scope Creep at the Gateway of India.

Talk to 12 islanders (press E near a speech bubble). Each one shares part of Omkar's story: his work at Zeus Learning, his internship, MAI Labs, CodeOS and Kotak Education Foundation volunteering, RAIT and the hackathons, and his podcast. The characters are made up, but everything they say comes from his résumé and website. Glowing LIFT pads under every crystal fly you straight up to it, so you can skip the platforming.

**Play:** https://oskorp.github.io/omkars-mumbai/

Built with Three.js (r128) in a single HTML file: day/night cycle, bloom, synthesised sound effects and music.

Controls: WASD to move or drive, Space to jump (drift when driving), Shift to sprint (boost), E to talk or read, F to get in or out of a vehicle, H for the horn, N for day/night, Esc for the menu. Touch controls on phones.
